About

I care about the parts of a website you only notice when they go wrong: the long wait for a page, the button a screen reader can't find, the form that forgets what you typed.

I started out building sites for local bands and now work as an independent front-end developer. Most projects begin with a slow or confusing site and end with something lighter, clearer and easier to maintain.

I work best side by side with designers, I test with real users and real devices, and I write documentation so your team can keep things running after I hand over.
