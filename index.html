01 · Interactive 3D

HELIOS, a solar company told in one 3D scene

A landing page where the scroll drives the day: the sun crosses the sky, dual-axis trackers turn to follow it and energy runs from the field to the house.

  • Procedural scene in Three.js: house, six trackers wired through a combiner to the inverter and batteries, terrain and drifting clouds
  • A camera framing for every section, plus a datasheet that appears when you hover a panel
  • Fog matched to the sky colour, so the distance dissolves with no horizon line
